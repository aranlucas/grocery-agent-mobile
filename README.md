# Grocery Agent

**Turn meal ideas into a grocery list you can shop and share.**

Grocery Agent is an AI grocery-planning app for iOS and Android. Describe the meals
you want to make or the budget you want to work with, review the resulting grocery
plan, and coordinate shopping with your household.

## From planning to shopping

Start with a request like:

> Plan five practical dinners for two people with a $100 grocery budget.

1. **Plan in chat.** Ask for meal ideas, recipes, or a grocery list, and refine
   the plan through conversation.
2. **Review your list.** Check the items, quantities, and matched products before
   shopping.
3. **Add to Kroger.** Connect your Kroger account for live products, prices, and
   cart actions. Confirm before sending matched items to your cart.

The Kroger connection is optional for planning and organizing your groceries.

## Keep what works for next time

- **Saved lists:** Reopen and edit personal or household shopping lists.
- **Saved recipes:** Keep ingredients, cooking instructions, servings, and notes
  together so you can reuse and adjust recipes.
- **Household sharing:** Create or join a household to share grocery lists and
  recipes with its members.
- **Chat history:** Resume an earlier conversation when your plans change.
